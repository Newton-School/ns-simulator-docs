---
title: "The Dual-Topology Rule defines a good question"
cluster: grading
tags: [concept, grading, anti-gaming]
---
A question is "authored" only when a reference design PASSES and a known gamed
shortcut FAILS on the intended axis. It is the executable definition of
discrimination - the architecture-world equivalent of hidden test cases. If the
gamed design passes, the question is under-constrained and rejected.
