---
title: "Performance is simulated; correctness is structural"
cluster: grading
tags: [concept, grading]
---
The engine grades what queueing physics can decide (latency, throughput, saturation)
via the rubric, and refuses to fake what it cannot (exactly-once, ordering) - those are
carried by topology structure plus a justification. Never a sim check for correctness.
