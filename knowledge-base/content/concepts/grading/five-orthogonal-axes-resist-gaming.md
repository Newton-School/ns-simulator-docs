---
title: "Five orthogonal grading axes resist gaming"
cluster: grading
tags: [concept, grading, anti-gaming]
---
A design is graded on Topology, Scale-fit, Simulation, Justification, and Budget.
Gaming one axis (e.g. tuning a metric) is caught by another (e.g. cost or structure),
so there is no single number to farm.
