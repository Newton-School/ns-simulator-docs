---
title: "Steady-state behavior differs from transient behavior"
cluster: simulation
tags: [concept, simulation]
---
Once queues fill to their working level, arrival and departure rates balance and
latency percentiles stabilize. Grading targets this steady state; the transient
fill is discarded via warmup.
